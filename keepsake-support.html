Rovely: Trip Photo Organizer · iPhone

Rovely helps you review trips, compare similar photos and sort screenshots on your iPhone, then create collections worth revisiting. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Getting started

Why does Rovely need access to my photos?
Rovely reads your library to group photos into trips, compare similar shots and find screenshots. Everything is analysed on your device, and nothing is uploaded.
I chose Limited access. Will Rovely still work?
Yes. Limited access works with the photos you selected. To include more, open Settings in Rovely and tap photo access, or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Rovely.

Reviewing and deleting

What happens when I confirm a removal?
The photos you marked move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos. iOS keeps them there for 30 days, so you can recover anything you change your mind about.
Does Rovely delete photos automatically?
No. Rovely only suggests; every removal needs your confirmation.
What does “Mark these as reviewed” do?
It clears the remaining similar photos from the review list without deleting them. You can undo it right away.

Rovely Pro

What is free?
Trip discovery, similar-photo review, the Screenshot Inbox, removal review, export, sharing, slideshows and monthly recaps are free. You can create up to two collections at no cost.
What does Pro include?
Rovely Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited collections. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Your existing collections and core review tools remain available without Pro.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in Rovely and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

Privacy

Do I need an account?
No. Rovely has no sign-in, no cloud library and no analytics.
Are my photos uploaded?
Never. All analysis stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
