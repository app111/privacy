Keepsake: Photo Declutter · iPhone

Keepsake helps you sort trips, similar photos and screenshots on your iPhone, then keep the moments worth revisiting. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Getting started

Why does Keepsake need access to my photos?
Keepsake reads your library to group photos into trips, compare similar shots and find screenshots. Everything is analysed on your device, and nothing is uploaded.
I chose Limited access. Will Keepsake still work?
Yes. Limited access works with the photos you selected. To include more, open Settings in Keepsake and tap photo access, or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Keepsake.

Reviewing and deleting

What happens when I confirm a cleanup?
The photos you marked move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos. iOS keeps them there for 30 days, so you can recover anything you change your mind about.
Does Keepsake delete photos automatically?
No. Keepsake only suggests; every cleanup needs your confirmation.
What does “Mark these as reviewed” do?
It clears the remaining similar photos from the review list without deleting them. You can undo it right away.

Keepsake Pro

What is free?
Trips, similar-photo review and the screenshot inbox are free. You can create up to two collections at no cost.
What does Pro include?
Keepsake Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited collections. There is no subscription and nothing renews.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in Keepsake and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

Privacy

Do I need an account?
No. Keepsake has no sign-in, no cloud library and no analytics.
Are my photos uploaded?
Never. All analysis stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
