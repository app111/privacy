隐私政策

最后更新：2026-08-30

「今天喝啥」是一款本地饮品记录工具。本政策只说明一件事：唯一会离开您设备的数据， 是您主动使用 AI 功能时被发送的内容，以及它的去向。

一、仅保存在本地的数据

饮品记录（名称、糖分、咖啡因、热量、花费等）、每日目标与偏好设置、小组件展示数据， 全部存储于您设备的本地安全存储中。我们没有服务器数据库，无法访问这些数据； 卸载应用即彻底删除。

二、发送至第三方 AI 服务的数据

仅当您主动点击 AI 功能（识别饮品、生成洞察报告）时，以下内容会经加密连接 （HTTPS）发送至智谱开放平台（北京智谱华章科技， 隐私政策）处理：

  • 您拍摄的饮品照片（压缩后传输，仅用于识别饮品名称与营养信息）；
  • 由应用在本地汇总的匿名统计数据（如"本周 7 杯、172g 糖"等数字）， 仅用于生成个性化建议。

我们不保存这些内容；请勿在照片中包含可识别个人身份的信息。

三、我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话等身份信息；
  • 无广告、无统计或广告 SDK、不跨应用追踪；
  • 不出售或共享您的数据（AI 处理除外，见第二条）。

四、通知与权限

提醒功能使用系统本地通知，不联网、可随时关闭；相机权限仅用于拍摄饮品照片， 可在系统设置中撤销。

五、未成年人

未成年人应在监护人同意下使用；我们不主动收集任何未成年人信息。

六、数据删除与更新

在应用内删除记录或卸载应用，即彻底删除全部本地数据；AI 服务商侧的处理 按其隐私政策执行。本政策如有更新，在本页发布并更新顶部日期。联系邮箱：niuniugede@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30

TodayDrink is a local-first drink logging tool. This policy explains one thing only: the only data that ever leaves your device is what you actively send while using an AI feature, and where it goes.

1. Data stored only on your device

Drink records (name, sugar, caffeine, calories, price and more), daily goals and preferences, and Home Screen widget data are all stored in your device's local secure storage. We run no server database and cannot access this data; uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

2. Data sent to third-party AI services

Only when you actively tap an AI feature (recognize a drink, generate an insight report) is the following sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the Zhipu Open Platform (Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology, privacy policy) for processing:

  • The drink photo you took (sent compressed, used only to identify the drink name and its nutrition);
  • Anonymous figures aggregated locally by the app (such as "7 cups this week, 172 g of sugar"), used only to generate personalized suggestions.

We never store this content; please avoid including personally identifiable information in photos.

3. What we never do

  • No sign-up, and we don't collect your name, email, phone number or any other identity information;
  • No ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking;
  • We don't sell or share your data (except the AI processing described in Section 2).

4. Notifications & permissions

Reminders use system local notifications — they never touch the network and can be turned off anytime. The camera permission is used only to photograph drinks and can be revoked in system Settings.

5. Minors

Minors should use the app with a guardian's consent; we do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

6. Data deletion & updates

Deleting records in the app, or uninstalling it, erases all local data permanently; processing on the AI provider's side follows its own privacy policy. Updates to this policy are published on this page with the date at the top updated. Contact: niuniugede@gmail.com
