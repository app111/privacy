Privacy Policy

Unarrow is a puzzle game. There is no account and no sign-in. This page says exactly what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and how to erase it.

1. Nothing leaves your device

Version 1.0 has no server. The app makes no network requests, so no data about you or your play is uploaded, and there is no copy of your progress anywhere we could look at — we have no way to look.

Specifically, Unarrow contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no third-party tracker and no crash reporter of its own, and it never asks for the advertising identifier or permission to track. You can turn your networking off entirely and the app behaves the same.

One thing we do not control: if you have iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › iPhone Analytics turned on with sharing enabled, iOS itself may send us a crash report when the app quits unexpectedly. Those reports come from the operating system, not from code in the app, and contain no puzzles you solved and nothing you wrote.

2. What is stored, and where

Everything the app knows about you lives in the app's own storage on this device:

  • Your Journey progress — which levels you cleared, your best moves and best time on each.
  • Every run you finish, including a step-by-step record of each move you made. This is what lets a score open back down to the move that produced it.
  • Your Brain Score and its five dimensions, and the samples used to draw their trend.
  • Your streak, today's Daily board result, and your settings.

None of it is tied to your name, your email, your Apple ID or your location, because the app never asks for any of those.

3. Your controls, inside the app

All three are under Profile › Settings:

  • Export your data — writes the complete local record (every run, every recorded move, every setting) to a JSON file you control. The app hands it to the iOS share sheet and keeps nothing back; it is never uploaded anywhere by us.
  • Reset all progress — clears everything the app stores on this device. The confirmation tells you how many runs and levels are about to go. Your purchase is not touched, since that belongs to your Apple ID rather than to this device.
  • Restore purchases — asks Apple, on this device, whether your Apple ID already owns Unarrow Pro.

Deleting the app from your device removes its local storage too.

4. Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device by iOS. Turning it on asks the system for permission; if you decline, nothing is scheduled. We are not told that a reminder fired, and there is nowhere for that information to go.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card number, your name or your billing address — the app only learns which products your Apple ID owns.

6. Children

Unarrow is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone, on any device, at all.

7. What Unarrow is not

Unarrow is a puzzle game. It describes how you solve puzzles. It is not a measure of intelligence and is not a medical or cognitive assessment.

8. If this changes

If a future version ever sends anything off your device, this page is rewritten to say what, to whom and why before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label is updated to match it. The version number this page describes is in the footer.
