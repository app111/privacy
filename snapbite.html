SnapBite Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SnapBite ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data when you use our iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

🔥 Our Core Promise

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Your food photos, nutrition logs, and health data stay on your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework and is never sent to our servers:

  • Meal logs (food items, portions, nutrition data)
  • Food photos (compressed and stored locally)
  • User profile (weight, goals, preferences)
  • Streak data and progress history

2. Data Sent to Third-Party AI APIs

When you take a photo of your food, the image is sent to a third-party AI service for recognition. Specifically:

  • Google Gemini API — receives your food photo for AI identification and nutrition estimation
  • DeepSeek API — receives your meal log text (food names and nutrition numbers, NOT photos) for generating weekly insights
  • OpenAI API (fallback only) — receives your food photo if Gemini is unavailable

Important: These AI providers process your data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control how they handle data after processing. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • DeepSeek: https://www.deepseek.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/privacy

We never store your food photos on any server. Photos are sent directly from your device to the AI API and discarded after processing.

3. Apple Health Integration

SnapBite integrates with Apple HealthKit to:

  • Write: Nutrition data (calories, protein, carbs, fat) for meals you log
  • Read: Your body weight (optional, to show weight trends)

This data is stored in your Apple Health database and is under your full control. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

4. Photos and Camera

We request access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or select food photos for AI recognition. We do not access, upload, or store photos beyond the meal you choose to log.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

All payments are processed by Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not see or store your payment information. Subscription management is handled entirely through your Apple ID.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:

  • ❌ Your name or email (unless you contact support)
  • ❌ Your location data
  • ❌ Your device identifiers for advertising
  • ❌ Your contact list
  • ❌ Usage analytics or tracking data
  • ❌ Your food photos on any server

7. Children's Privacy

SnapBite does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app does not require account registration or personal information.

8. Data Retention

All your data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed. We have no backup of your data because we never have it.

9. Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, you have full control:

  • Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Revoke HealthKit access in Settings
  • Revoke Camera/Photo access in Settings
  • Cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings

10. Open Source

SnapBite is built with open standards and Apple frameworks. We use no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking libraries.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
