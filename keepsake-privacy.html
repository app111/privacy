Privacy Policy

Keepsake: Photo Declutter · Effective 9 November 2026

Keepsake is built to work entirely on your iPhone. You do not need an account, your photos are never uploaded, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

1. Information Keepsake processes on your device

With your permission, Keepsake reads your photo library so it can group photos into trips, find similar shots and separate screenshots. This includes photo metadata such as the time and location a photo was taken. All of this processing happens locally on your iPhone.

Keepsake stores small analysis records (for example, visual fingerprints used to compare photos) in the app's local storage so that repeat scans are faster. These records stay on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No copies or uploads of your photos or videos.
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or tracking across apps.
  • No transmission of your location or photo metadata to us or to third parties.

3. Purchases

Keepsake Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and purchase records; we do not receive your card details or billing information. You can restore a previous purchase at any time from Settings inside the app.

4. Deleting photos

Keepsake never removes a photo on its own. When you confirm a cleanup, selected photos are moved to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before permanent deletion. You can recover them there at any time during that period.

5. Data retention and deletion

Analysis records and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data. Because we do not operate a server or maintain an account for you, there is no server-side data to request or delete.

6. Children

Keepsake does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
