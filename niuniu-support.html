牛牛浏览器（NiuNiu Browser）· 适用于版本 1.3

这是一款把数据留在自己手机上的浏览器。遇到问题先看下面的清单，多数情况可以在应用内解决。

一、常见问题

广告过滤把正常内容拦掉了怎么办？
浏览页右上角盾牌 → 广告拦截详情 → 关闭「广告过滤」即可放行本页。旧版本保存的自定义拦截域名也能在同一页查看和移除。
「本页拦截」显示 0，是不是没生效？
不一定。计数只统计命中广告域名的资源加载失败，页面本身没有这类请求时就是 0，可对照「累计拦截」确认规则在工作。
网页视频调不出播放器 / 倍速没反应？
播放器只在检测到 HTML5 <video> 时出现。使用自研播放内核或 DRM 加密流的站点（部分长视频站）无法接管，这是系统 WebKit 的限制。
清晰度为什么只有部分视频能选？
清晰度只对 HLS 自适应流（m3u8）有意义，iOS 侧能控制的是峰值码率上限；单文件视频没有可切换的变体，这时面板就不显示。
下载中断了会重新下吗？
不会。下载支持断点续传：切到后台会自动暂停，回到应用自动继续；手动暂停后可从断点接着下。
私密空间忘了面容 / 换机怎么办？
可用设备密码进入。文件与钥匙串密钥都只在本机，不提供云端备份或找回，卸载应用或恢复出厂会永久丢失，请先移出重要文件。
无痕标签真的不留痕迹吗？
不写浏览历史；无痕标签使用独立的非持久站点数据，关闭标签即连同 Cookie 与缓存一起丢弃。书签只有你手动点「收藏」才会保存。网站侧仍能通过它自己的日志识别访问，这不在本应用可控范围内。
英文界面为什么没有天气？
天气与定位目前只在中文界面提供。英文界面不会请求定位权限，其余功能不受影响。

Support

NiuNiu Browser · Applies to version 1.3

A browser that keeps your data on your own phone. Check the list below first; most issues can be resolved inside the app.

1. Common questions

Ad blocking blocked something it shouldn't.
On a page, tap the shield in the top-right, open Ad Blocking Details, and turn off Ad Blocking to allow this page. Custom blocking domains saved by earlier versions can also be viewed and removed on the same screen.
"Blocked on this page" shows 0. Is blocking working?
Not necessarily. The counter only counts failed resource loads that match blocked domains. If the page makes no such requests, it stays 0. Check the cumulative count to confirm the rules are active.
No player for web video, or speed controls do nothing.
The player appears only when an HTML5 <video> element is detected. Sites with custom players or DRM-protected streams (some long-form video sites) cannot be taken over; this is a WebKit limitation.
Why can I change quality for only some videos?
Quality selection only applies to adaptive HLS streams (m3u8), where the app controls the peak bitrate. Single-file videos have no switchable variants, so the option is hidden.
Will an interrupted download resume?
Yes. Downloads support resuming: they pause automatically when the app goes to the background and continue when you return. After a manual pause, you can resume from where it stopped.
I forgot my Face ID, or I switched phones. What about Private Space?
You can unlock with your device passcode. Files and the Keychain key exist only on this device, and there is no cloud backup or recovery. Deleting the app or erasing the device permanently loses them, so move important files out first.
Are private tabs really private?
They do not write browsing history, and they use separate non-persistent site data; closing the tab discards cookies and cache with it. Bookmarks are saved only when you tap Save. Websites can still identify visits in their own server logs, which is outside this app's control.
Why is there no weather in the English interface?
Weather and location are currently available in the Chinese interface only. The English interface does not request location permission; everything else works the same.
