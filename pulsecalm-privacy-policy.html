Privacy Policy

PulseCalm — Biofeedback Meditation

Your privacy matters to us. PulseCalm is designed with privacy-first principles — your health data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

PulseCalm accesses the following data through Apple HealthKit:

  • Heart rate data (during meditation sessions only)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)

This data is read from Apple Health with your explicit permission and is used solely to provide real-time biofeedback during meditation sessions and to calculate your Calm Score.

We also store locally: meditation session duration/timestamps and app preferences.

How We Use Your Data

Your health data is used exclusively to:

  • Display real-time heart rate during meditation
  • Calculate your personalized Calm Score
  • Track your meditation progress and streaks
  • Provide insights about your relaxation patterns

We never use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than improving your meditation experience within the app.

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your meditation history, preferences, and health metrics never leave your device.

If you use iCloud, your meditation data may be synced across your Apple devices through Apple's encrypted iCloud infrastructure. We do not have access to this data.

We do not operate any servers that store your personal data.

Third-Party Services

PulseCalm uses the following Apple frameworks:

  • HealthKit — for heart rate data access
  • StoreKit — for subscription management
  • WidgetKit — for home/lock screen widgets

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not share your data with any third parties.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Revoke HealthKit access anytime in Settings → Privacy → Health
  • Delete all meditation history from within the app
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Request data export by contacting us

Since we don't collect data on our servers, there is nothing to delete on our end.

Children's Privacy

PulseCalm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
